QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Everything you need to know.

Does ASHTi SVV replace ETABS?

No. It is a companion verification and reporting platform. ETABS remains the structural analysis environment.

Which versions are supported?

Separate editions target ETABS 22.7 and ETABS 20.0.0. Verify your exact installation with the developer.

Are engineering results automatically approved?

No. All results require independent review by a qualified engineer, including model assumptions and applicable code provisions.

Can I purchase directly on this website?

No. Contact the developer on WhatsApp to confirm payment, licensing and activation details.

Why are some report downloads unavailable?

Sample files must be uploaded into the repository's assets/excel folder using the exact listed filenames.
