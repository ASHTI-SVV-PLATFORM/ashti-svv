FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Is ASHTi SVV a replacement for ETABS?

No. ASHTi SVV is intended as a companion platform for supported data extraction and verification workflows. ETABS remains the structural analysis software.

Which ETABS versions are supported?

Separate editions are intended for ETABS 20.0.0 and ETABS 22.7. Contact the developer to confirm compatibility with your installation.

Does the website process payments?

No. Contact the developer to confirm the license, payment instructions, and activation procedure.

Can I use the results without engineering review?

No. Software outputs must be reviewed by a qualified engineer. The engineer is responsible for model assumptions, applicable codes, interpretation, and final design decisions.
